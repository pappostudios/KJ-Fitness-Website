Four landing page directions

Each direction is a working prototype with the full page in English and Hebrew. All four are built from the logo teal #26B4C0, meet WCAG AA contrast, and send every call to action to WhatsApp.

How to review
  • Scroll inside the desktop frames to see each full page.
  • Open a prototype to try the EN / עב toggle, the menu, the services and the consultation modal.
  • A small Events switch in the corner of each prototype shows the populated and empty states. The two events shown are sample data.
  • The Hebrew copy is a draft for Kirsten to review.

Studio Journal

Light & editorial

A calm, book-like serif and generous white space make KJ read as a considered, grown-up practice, which suits 35+ professionals weighing their options.

  • LayoutSplit hero with a big serif headline and a tinted panel. Arch-framed portrait, numbered pillars on hairline rules, events as a dated list.
  • K→J sweepSolid logo-teal stroke with a hairline echo in the hero panel. It mirrors in Hebrew.
  • ServicesRefined accordion next to a sticky intro. Each row shows its price, and the chevron points to the inline end.
  • Primary CTATeal 700 pill with white label, in the header and hero and in every service.

Logo Blocks

Teal block

It takes the logo literally: flat teal fields, square tiles and heavy Rubik type make the brand instantly recognisable and very direct about prices and next steps.

  • LayoutFull-bleed teal hero with a 2×2 grid of square tiles, a square portrait with a teal stamp, and pillars as four coloured squares on a dark field.
  • K→J sweepNot used. The motif here is the logo's square format, and the logo appears full-size as a tile.
  • ServicesA tile grid with every price and inclusion visible at once, so nothing is hidden behind a click.
  • Primary CTASquare ink button (#0A3236, white label) with a hard offset shadow on hover.

Evening Session

Deep teal premium

Dark, quiet and premium, it feels like a private studio after hours. That suits 1:1 and corporate clients, and the logo teal is kept for the moments that matter.

  • Primary CTALogo-teal pill with ink label (6.36:1). The mobile menu is a drawer from the inline end.

D

Momentum

Athletic motion

The K→J sweep becomes movement itself. Diagonals, curved section edges and a rising stair of pillars bring energy without neon or bro-gym clichés.

  • LayoutA giant sweep crosses the hero and draws itself in on load. A slow pillar ticker, a slanted portrait, ticket-style events and curved section edges follow.
  • K→J sweepDominant. It appears in the hero, as curved section breaks, behind the quote and in the empty events state, and every instance mirrors in Hebrew.
  • ServicesA side-by-side comparison of the three coaching plans (Hybrid marked “Best of both”), plus two wide banners for the class and corporate.
  • Primary CTASlanted logo-teal button with ink label. On mobile a sticky WhatsApp bar stays in reach, and the modal opens as a bottom sheet.
